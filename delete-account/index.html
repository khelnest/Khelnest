Account and Data Deletion

KhelNest / com.khelnest.app / Last updated: 5 October 2026

This page explains how to request KhelNest account and data deletion without installing the app, what the current processor removes, and what remains stored. Android package: com.khelnest.app. Official website: khelnest.in. Contact us: Contact us.

Email an account deletion request

Alternatively, use Contact us. Opening the email link does not submit a request.

On this page

1. Request without the app

Use the Mail link on this page with subject "KhelNest account deletion request" from the email associated with your KhelNest account. Ask to delete your account and associated personal data. A reason is optional. We may confirm ownership by replying to that email. Do not send your Google password, KhelNest password, OTP, authentication token or payout details.

A mail link opens your email application; it does not itself submit or complete deletion. If you cannot access that account email, use Contact us at Contact us to discuss a proportionate ownership check.

2. Request inside the app

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Review the balance and payout warning, acknowledge the consequences and confirm the request. The queue records a 48-hour cancellation window. You can cancel an open request from the same screen. Earliest eligibility is not a promised completion time, and uninstalling is not an account-deletion request.

3. Money and local practice

Resolve pending payouts before closing the account; a payout hold must have a documented resolution and cannot justify an indefinite deletion delay. Unredeemed NestCoins are forfeited when account closure is completed. Deleting a KhelNest account does not delete your Google account. Installation-local puzzle-practice state and app settings are separate from the online account; clear app storage to remove them when appropriate, after considering any local progress you want to retain.

4. Current app-data processing

The processor can clear profile name/photo and demographics, rotate the referral code, delete personal notifications/read receipts and queued pushes, remove device signals and registered notification devices, detach first-party analytics identity links, and redact payout destinations/contact phones on settled or rejected requests.

Financial/game/security/audit records remain linked to the retained account identifier. Automated processing is currently disabled in the reviewed deployment.

5. Incomplete erasure and retention

The current processor does not erase the Supabase Auth user, email, provider metadata or authentication sessions and does not erase already-sent Google Analytics data. A queue status of "completed" is not complete account erasure. The full account/auth/provider erasure workflow, justified retention periods and a request-handling timeframe still need to be completed and verified.

No fixed completion timeframe is currently committed; use Contact us to discuss the status of your request.

6. Other privacy choices

You can edit/clear optional profile answers, disable Usage analytics, mute notification categories and use applicable ad privacy controls without closing the account. These controls stop or limit future processing; they are not a promise to erase historical provider records. Questions and access/correction/deletion requests can be sent through Contact us at Contact us.
