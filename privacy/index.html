Privacy Policy

KhelNest / com.khelnest.app / Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes KhelNest, the Android application com.khelnest.app, and its KhelNest Supabase backend. KhelNest currently supports eligible adults in India (IN) only. Official website: khelnest.in. Privacy inquiries: Contact us at Contact us. The public policy address is Privacy Policy. The updated consent, retention and erasure controls require backend rollout before they can be relied on as live service behavior; current limitations are explained below.

1. Account data

Google sign-in provides an account identifier, email address, display name and profile-photo URL. Supabase Authentication stores the app account and authentication/session records; the profile uses the identifying details needed to operate the account. We never receive your Google password. The user app currently supports Google sign-in only; it does not offer an email/password sign-in form.

Authentication and hosting providers may process IP addresses, request metadata and security logs when you connect.

1a. Profile and onboarding answers

At sign-up we ask for your date of birth and optional gender and preference answers. The server calculates your age from the date you supply; this is self-reported information, not a document or identity check. The date is used for the 18+ eligibility gate and age-band reporting. Optional answers support profile preferences, product analysis and relevant reminders.

KhelNest is offered to users in India and does not ask for your country or location. A profile created before this change may still hold a country you chose then; it is used to check approved reward markets for free daily bonuses and CPX surveys when enabled, and it is not precise device location or verified residence. You can edit your profile and clear optional answers in Settings, then Account, then Your profile.

Answering the optional questions is not a condition of using the app. A one-time setup reward is not payment for your data, and does not authorize undisclosed data sharing.

2. Economy and operational data

We record server-verified game sessions, claims, referrals, coin-ledger entries, payout state, and fraud signals to operate rewards, prevent abuse, resolve support issues, and meet financial and legal obligations. These records are required to provide the service and are not controlled by the optional analytics toggle.

3. Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics is OFF on a fresh install. If you enable it in Settings, first-party Supabase analytics and Google Analytics for Firebase receive screen views, session timing, feature/game usage, operational ad events and conversion steps. Firebase receives the internal app user ID while signed in, so these events are pseudonymous and may be associated with an account; they are not automatically anonymous.

We do not put an email, payout handle, password or authentication token into custom analytics events. Firebase may also process app-instance identifiers, device/app metadata, diagnostics and IP-derived general location under its service configuration. Disabling Usage analytics stops future optional collection and clears the app's pending upload queue; it does not automatically erase records already sent to Google or Supabase.

The analytics setting does not disable required account, reward or security records, or independently stop AdMob's own processing.

4. Installation and integrity

The app generates a random identifier scoped to this installation and sends it through an authenticated request for anti-abuse checks. Our server stores a one-way digest linked to the app account. Hashing does not make account-linked data anonymous. This app-generated value is not Android ID, advertising ID or IMEI. Firebase and AdMob have their own identifiers, described separately below.

The reviewed client does not obtain a real Play Integrity attestation; it must not be described as identity or device verification.

5. Payout details

When you request a redemption we collect the selected payout destination, such as a UPI id, PayPal email or voucher-delivery email, and the amount, status and request reference. An optional UPI contact phone number can help resolve a failed transfer; it is not required and is not used for marketing. Payout requests and payments are processed manually.

Authorized payout operators and the chosen payment or voucher provider receive the information necessary to fulfill or investigate the request. Operator queues mask sensitive details where supported; access to full payout information remains privileged. The app does not collect card details for an in-app purchase or accept deposits.

5a. Payout-proof feed and optional sharing

The recent-paid-payout feed is available to other signed-in users. It displays a partially masked profile name, payout method, NestCoin amount, currency amount and settlement time; its response also includes the redemption record identifier. It does not include the payout destination, contact phone, email, settlement reference or operator notes.

Masking a name does not make the record anonymous. The updated sharing control is OFF by default and publishes a proof only after explicit opt-in. Turning it off hides proofs from future feed requests; it cannot recall copies already seen or saved by another person. Sharing never awards NestCoin or improves payout eligibility.

The backend consent migration and updated app must be deployed together before this control is effective. Until rollout is verified, the previous feed must not be treated as consent-controlled; this disclosure is not a substitute for required consent.

6. Ads and third parties

Banner, native and interstitial ads are provided by Google AdMob. Its SDK and advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and protect ads according to the applicable consent choices and Google's policies. Where consent is required we ask through Google's consent form before requesting ads, and privacy options are available in Settings when Google makes them applicable.

The current app does not offer rewarded videos. Viewing or clicking an ad does not earn NestCoin.

6a. Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a Firebase installation identifier and registration token, together with app/device metadata, to deliver notifications. KhelNest stores the token linked to your account and your category preferences for payout updates, reminders and announcements. We use the token for notification delivery, never for advertising or profiling.

Updated survey reminders are OFF until you opt in. When CPX and the schedule are approved and enabled, they can be sent every two daytime hours, at most six engagement reminders in a rolling 24 hours, subject to quiet hours, account eligibility and recent app activity. A reminder is not a promise that a survey is available or earns NestCoin.

You can mute categories in Settings, then Notifications, or revoke notification permission in Android settings. The app attempts to retire backend registration and the native FCM token on sign-out or observed permission denial. Already delivered or queued messages may not be recalled. Registered notification devices are removed when authorized app-data erasure runs.

Actual delivery depends on the device, network and system settings.

6b. Paid surveys and partner tasks

CPX Research surveys are an optional activity offered only to eligible accounts in owner-approved markets, currently India (IN) only; other partner placements remain disabled in the reviewed backend. Before the survey browser opens, the app shows CPX's privacy notice and participation terms and waits for you to continue.

When you continue, CPX receives an app user identifier to attribute completions, and may collect survey answers, IP address, browser/device details and other information disclosed by CPX. The CPX session integration provides the app user ID and CPX app ID; it does not supply your Google password, payout destination or the onboarding date of birth.

CPX server callbacks return completion, reward, screen-out and reversal references to KhelNest. We store them with the conversion record, including any held or reversed status and any amount that could not be recovered, for ledger reconciliation, fraud prevention and support; section 7 applies to these records. We also store each credit's credit and unlock times and its locked or released coin amount to enforce an independent 48-hour withdrawal hold for CPX credits.

This hold is separate from payout-request reservations and account-deletion grace periods. Optional participation is not blanket consent for unrelated data use. No Google ad may be added over another party's monetized survey without an allowed integration.

6c. Service providers and local data

Supabase provides account authentication, database, storage and server functions. Google provides sign-in, AdMob, Firebase Messaging and optional Firebase Analytics. Their providers may process data outside your country; the reviewed Supabase project is hosted in Mumbai, India. The app stores sign-in session information, settings, a pending analytics queue and puzzle-practice state locally.

Offline practice boards and completion counts are installation-local, shared by people using the device, and are not account balances or cloud backups. App-data clearing or uninstall can remove them. The user app does not request location, contacts, SMS, camera or microphone access; IP-derived general location and SDK identifiers are separate processing and are not excluded by that statement.

6d. Optional WhatsApp channel

The app and website link to the KhelNest WhatsApp channel for updates. Opening the link hands you to WhatsApp or a browser, where the provider's own privacy terms apply. KhelNest uses no WhatsApp membership API, does not verify channel membership, send your app account or payout details with this link, or award NestCoin for joining.

You may tick the joined checkbox and confirm to stop invitations on that installation. Until you confirm, the invitation may return on an eligible Home visit or app resume after at least 24 hours; opening or dismissing the link does not confirm membership. The app stores your self-confirmation and the last invitation time locally.

The Settings link remains available; clearing app data or reinstalling can reset these local preferences. WhatsApp may process your account and interaction data when you use its service.

7. Retention

The previous first-party raw-analytics setting was 180 days, a configured window rather than evidence of automatic purging. The updated workflow requires an owner-approved duration and justification before erasure or retention cleanup can execute. It records the retained-account deadline, supports documented legal holds, and has bounded cleanup for expired raw analytics, per-user analytics and erasure receipts.

Restricted pseudonymous financial/security records may be retained for payout reconciliation, disputes and fraud prevention, with an Auth tombstone until the approved deadline; they are not automatically anonymous. A separate verified purge removes the retained account after that deadline when no hold applies. This does not erase prior disclosures or independently prove backup, platform-log or provider deletion.

These exceptions, justified durations and provider/backup handling still require operator review and deployment verification. No production financial-record duration or fixed completion deadline is currently committed.

8. Account and data deletion

Request deletion in Settings, then Account, then Delete account, or use the Mail link at Account deletion from the account email address without installing the app. Do not send a password, OTP or payment details. The request records a 48-hour grace window; earliest eligibility is not a promised completion date.

You may cancel until irreversible processing starts. The reviewed deployment still has automated processing disabled. The updated workflow clears personal profile/demographic details, personal notifications, queued pushes and device signals; removes account-owned first-party raw analytics; hides payout proofs; and redacts settled payout destinations/contact phones.

It then uses supported Supabase Auth user deletion to redact email, provider metadata and identities and remove sessions, with database verification before progressing. Google Analytics deletion requests are tracked as submitted, not as verified completion; app-instance coverage and other partner data require separate review.

Completion requires the Auth checks and required provider evidence. Historical completion from the previous processor is not proof of full account erasure. Pending payouts and locked balances block erasure and must receive a documented resolution, not indefinite delay. Any remaining NestCoin is forfeited when erasure starts.

Restricted retained records follow section 7; your Google account itself is unaffected. Deployment, request-handling timeframes and end-to-end provider fulfillment remain to be verified.

9. Security and your choices

Account-specific database access is restricted through row-level security and server authorization. Sensitive operator actions are audited. Authentication and service requests use HTTPS, and reward/payout decisions are server-authoritative. No system is absolutely secure. You can request access, correction, deletion or clarification of retention through Contact us at Contact us; we may verify ownership through the account email without requesting your password.

Disabling optional analytics, notifications or survey participation is separate from deleting the account. Statutory privacy rights remain applicable in your jurisdiction.

10. Adults, changes and contact

KhelNest earning/redemption is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. The age check is based on a date of birth the user supplies. Report an underage account or a privacy concern through Contact us at Contact us. Material policy changes will be dated and communicated where required.

Publishing this policy does not mean the currently incomplete deletion and retention workflows have been completed.
