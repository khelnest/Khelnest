Privacy Policy

Android application com.khelnest.app | Review date: 5 October 2026

Unpublished draft. Not a release-readiness or platform-approval certificate.

KhelNest Privacy Policy. Draft review: 5 October 2026. This policy describes the reviewed Android application com.khelnest.app and its KhelNest Supabase backend. Official website: https://khelnest.in/. Privacy inquiries: Contact us at https://khelnest.in/contact/. Operator approval, retention periods, public hosting and complete deletion fulfillment are required before publication. The public policy address is https://khelnest.in/privacy/.

1. Account data

Google sign-in provides an account identifier, email address, display name and profile-photo URL. Supabase Authentication stores the app account and authentication/session records; the profile uses the identifying details needed to operate the account. We never receive your Google password. Email/password access is also supported for named accounts: Supabase stores the email address and a password hash rather than a readable password. Authentication and hosting providers may process IP addresses, request metadata and security logs when you connect.

1a. Profile and onboarding answers

At sign-up we ask for your date of birth and optional gender, country and preference answers. The server calculates your age from the date you supply; this is self-reported information, not a document or identity check. The date is used for the 18+ eligibility gate and age-band reporting. Optional answers support profile preferences, product analysis and relevant reminders. Country selection is not precise device location or verified residence. You can edit your profile and clear optional answers in Settings, then Account, then Your profile. Answering the optional questions is not a condition of using the app. A one-time setup reward is not payment for your data, and does not authorize undisclosed data sharing.

2. Economy and operational data

We record server-verified game sessions, claims, referrals, coin-ledger entries, payout state, and fraud signals to operate rewards, prevent abuse, resolve support issues, and meet financial and legal obligations. These records are required to provide the service and are not controlled by the optional analytics toggle.

3. Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics is OFF on a fresh install. If you enable it in Settings, first-party Supabase analytics and Google Analytics for Firebase receive screen views, session timing, feature/game usage, operational ad events and conversion steps. Firebase receives the internal app user ID while signed in, so these events are pseudonymous and may be associated with an account; they are not automatically anonymous. We do not put an email, payout handle, password or authentication token into custom analytics events. Firebase may also process app-instance identifiers, device/app metadata, diagnostics and IP-derived general location under its service configuration. Disabling Usage analytics stops future optional collection and clears the app's pending upload queue; it does not automatically erase records already sent to Google or Supabase. The analytics setting does not disable required account, reward or security records, or independently stop AdMob's own processing.

4. Installation and integrity

The app generates a random identifier scoped to this installation and sends it through an authenticated request for anti-abuse checks. Our server stores a one-way digest linked to the app account. Hashing does not make account-linked data anonymous. This app-generated value is not Android ID, advertising ID or IMEI. Firebase and AdMob have their own identifiers, described separately below. The reviewed client does not obtain a real Play Integrity attestation; it must not be described as identity or device verification.

5. Payout details

When you request a redemption we collect the selected payout destination, such as a UPI id, PayPal email or voucher-delivery email, and the amount, status and request reference. An optional UPI contact phone number can help resolve a failed transfer; it is not required and is not used for marketing. Authorized payout operators and the chosen payment or voucher provider receive the information necessary to fulfill or investigate the request. Operator queues mask sensitive details where supported; access to full payout information remains privileged. The app does not collect card details for an in-app purchase or accept deposits.

6. Ads and third parties

Banner, native and interstitial ads are provided by Google AdMob. Its SDK and advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and protect ads according to the applicable consent choices and Google's policies. Where consent is required we ask through Google's consent form before requesting ads, and privacy options are available in Settings when Google makes them applicable. The current app does not offer rewarded videos. Viewing or clicking an ad does not earn NestCoins.

6a. Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a Firebase installation identifier and registration token, together with app/device metadata, to deliver notifications. KhelNest stores the registration token linked to your account and your category preferences for payout updates, reminders and announcements. We use it for notification delivery, never for advertising or profiling. You can mute categories in Settings, then Notifications, or revoke notification permission in Android settings. The app attempts to retire the device token on sign-out or observed permission denial. Retiring delivery does not mean all historical records have been deleted. Registered notification devices and their tokens are removed by the app-data deletion processor when that processor actually runs.

6b. Paid surveys and partner tasks

CPX Research and other partner placements are currently disabled in the reviewed backend. If enabled and you choose to open one, the partner receives an app user identifier to attribute completions, and may collect survey answers, IP address, browser/device details and other information disclosed by that partner. The current CPX session integration provides the app user ID and CPX app ID; it does not supply your Google password, payout destination or the onboarding date of birth. Provider callbacks return completion, reward and reversal references to KhelNest for ledger reconciliation. A provider's own privacy notice and participation terms must be shown and reviewed before activation; optional participation is not blanket consent for unrelated data use. No Google ad may be added over another party's monetized survey without an allowed integration.

6c. Service providers and local data

Supabase provides account authentication, database, storage and server functions. Google provides sign-in, AdMob, Firebase Messaging and optional Firebase Analytics. Their providers may process data outside your country; the reviewed Supabase project is hosted in Mumbai, India. The app stores sign-in session information, settings, a pending analytics queue and puzzle-practice state locally. Offline practice boards and completion counts are installation-local, shared by people using the device, and are not account balances or cloud backups. App-data clearing or uninstall can remove them. The user app does not request location, contacts, SMS, camera or microphone access; IP-derived general location and SDK identifiers are separate processing and are not excluded by that statement.

7. Retention

The first-party raw-analytics setting is currently 180 days, but this is a configured window, not evidence of automatic purging: no scheduled raw-analytics purge was present in the reviewed deployment. Historical aggregates, ledger/game records, payout status, security records and deletion/audit records remain stored for operational reporting, payout reconciliation and fraud prevention. The operator must define and enforce justified retention periods before release, including the retained Auth identity, third-party data and backups. There is no verified deletion deadline or financial-record retention duration to promise in this draft. Data already processed by Google or a partner follows the relevant provider's controls as well as applicable deletion requests.

8. Account and data deletion

Request deletion in Settings, then Account, then Delete account, or use the Mail link at https://khelnest.in/delete-account/ from the account email address without installing the app. Do not send a password, OTP or payment details. The in-app request records a 48-hour cancellation window; that date is earliest eligibility, not a verified completion deadline. The current deployment has automated processing disabled. When an authorized operator runs the current app-data processor, it removes profile name/photo, date of birth, gender, country and onboarding answers; rotates the referral code; removes personal notifications, queued pushes, registered notification devices and device signals; removes first-party analytics identity links; and redacts settled/rejected payout destinations and optional contact phones. It blocks the retained profile and keeps account-linked financial, game, security and audit records. It does not erase the Supabase Auth user, email, provider metadata or sessions, nor does it perform Google Analytics erasure. A completed queue status is therefore not proof of complete account deletion. A verified end-to-end erasure/justified-retention process is required before release. Pending payouts need an explicit settlement or cancellation decision, not indefinite delay. Unredeemed NestCoins are forfeited when account closure is completed; your Google account itself is unaffected.

9. Security and your choices

Account-specific database access is restricted through row-level security and server authorization. Sensitive operator actions are audited. Authentication and service requests use HTTPS, and reward/payout decisions are server-authoritative. No system is absolutely secure. You can request access, correction, deletion or clarification of retention through Contact us at https://khelnest.in/contact/; we may verify ownership through the account email without requesting your password. Disabling optional analytics, notifications or survey participation is separate from deleting the account. Statutory privacy rights remain applicable in your jurisdiction.

10. Adults, changes and contact

KhelNest earning/redemption is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. The age check is based on a date of birth the user supplies. Report an underage account or a privacy concern through Contact us at https://khelnest.in/contact/. Material policy changes must be dated and communicated where required. This draft must not be submitted as an approved or fully implemented privacy/deletion policy.
