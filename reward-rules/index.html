How earning & payouts work

Android application com.khelnest.app | Review date: 5 October 2026

Unpublished draft. Not a release-readiness or platform-approval certificate.

KhelNest Reward Rules. Draft review: 5 October 2026. These disclosures do not establish Google Play or legal approval. The operator must finalize permitted markets, payout timeframes and selection-method disclosures before publishing the real-world reward program.

We want to be upfront about how this app works.

Earning NestCoins. Eligible game results, daily activities, free daily bonuses and qualifying referrals may earn NestCoins. Each activity has server-managed rules, qualifying events and daily caps. Account setup, milestones or gifts can also create a ledger credit where explicitly configured. Practice-only play does not create authoritative rewards. Partner surveys/tasks are currently disabled; a future provider credit requires verified completion and may be reversed for rejection or fraud under disclosed rules.

Ads do not pay coins. Rewarded videos, including ads for extra hearts, puzzle moves, hints or daily bonus attempts, are disabled in the current app. Banner, native and interstitial ads may appear, but ad views and clicks do not credit NestCoins or unlock a payout. You are not asked to watch an ad to receive a reward you have already earned. Any future rewarded benefit must be isolated from redeemable gameplay and cannot be converted into money or gift cards.

Free to play. Games and the daily bonus surfaces are always free to enter. You never spend or stake coins to play, and you can never lose coins by playing. Coins leave your balance in only one way that you choose: when YOU request a payout.

Variable bonuses and selection. Wheel, Scratch Card and Surprise Box outcomes are chosen on the server using configured reward tables. Visual animations reveal a result; they are not an independent proof of randomness or entitlement. Possible amounts, any zero outcome, weights/probabilities or other selection variables, limits and reset times must be accessible before public participation. This draft does not supply unverified probabilities. Free entry alone does not decide whether a cash-convertible mechanic is allowed by Google Play or local law, and this is not presented as a qualifying-transaction loyalty program.

No contests or tournaments. Players are never ranked against each other, and no reward here depends on beating anyone else. There is no leaderboard, no prize pool and no competition of any kind — what you earn depends only on your own play, within the published daily caps.

Payouts. The Wallet shows available NestCoins, locked amounts, the conversion rate, enabled payout methods and the minimum payout threshold. Depending on configured availability, methods include UPI transfer, PayPal or brand vouchers; do not assume bank-account transfer is available without a supported form. A request locks coins and is reviewed manually. Payouts are reviewed and are not guaranteed; rejected or failed requests follow the server's refund transitions. Google Play credit and Google Play gift cards are not offered. We do not promise any particular income. Published processing windows, fees and eligible countries must be finalized before release; a timeout or unresolved request is not permission to submit duplicate payouts.

Age requirement. You must be at least 18 years old to earn or redeem. During sign-up you enter your date of birth and our servers work out your age from it — the app on your device cannot declare you old enough. A date below the minimum is refused and nothing is stored, and earning and redemption stay blocked until an accepted date of birth is on file.

No rewards for reviews. We will never ask you to rate or review the app for coins. Rating us is always optional and unrewarded.

Questions and disputes. Use Contact us at https://khelnest.in/contact/ with your transaction/request reference and a description. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens. Google does not sponsor, guarantee or endorse KhelNest rewards. The operator must keep prior earned obligations and pending payouts accounted for when rules or backend projects change.
