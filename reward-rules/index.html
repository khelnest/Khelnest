How earning & payouts work

KhelNest / com.khelnest.app / Last updated: 28 October 2026

These KhelNest Reward Rules describe eligible activities, NestCoin and payout conditions for eligible adults in India (IN) only. They do not establish Google Play or legal approval. Daily-bonus participation requires current owner-approved rules. CPX participation follows the current configured eligibility and qualification rules without a separate manual owner-approval step. Payout requests and payments are handled manually; there is no automatic payment when a survey withdrawal hold expires.

Earning NestCoin

Eligible game results, daily activities, free daily bonuses and qualifying referrals may earn NestCoin. Each activity has server-managed rules, qualifying events and daily caps. Account setup, milestones or gifts can also create a ledger credit where explicitly configured. Practice-only play does not create authoritative rewards.

Paid surveys from CPX Research are optional and offered only to eligible accounts in owner-approved markets, currently India (IN) only; other partner tasks remain disabled. CPX's privacy notice, its participation terms and these rules are shown before you continue. A survey credits NestCoin only after CPX's server confirms a qualifying completion, screen-out reward or bonus, converted under the approved rules; opening a survey or closing the survey browser does not.

A completion reported while your account is not eligible or not active is recorded without a credit. CPX may reverse a completion for rejection or fraud: its survey-held coins are cancelled or the credit is deducted from available coins, the balance never goes below zero, and any amount that cannot be recovered is recorded for review.

Survey availability depends on CPX, your account and your region. Verified CPX rewards follow the approved conversion rate without the generic other-partner credit cap. Each CPX credit is included in your total balance immediately but remains survey-held for 48 hours from its server-recorded credit time. Each credit has its own unlock time; a new survey does not reset older credits.

Once due, the server releases that credit for payout requests. This is not a rule limiting you to one withdrawal every 48 hours. Non-survey app earnings have no survey hold and can be included in payout requests whenever the normal minimum amount, account eligibility and payout limits are met. Payouts are processed manually; expiry of a survey hold is not an automatic payment or a promised payout-processing deadline.

Reversal rules still apply after a survey hold expires.

Ads do not pay coins

Rewarded videos, including ads for extra hearts, puzzle moves, hints or daily bonus attempts, are disabled in the current app. Banner, native and interstitial ads may appear, but ad views and clicks do not credit NestCoin or unlock a payout. You are not asked to watch an ad to receive a reward you have already earned. Any future rewarded benefit must be isolated from redeemable gameplay and cannot be converted into money or gift cards.

Free to play

Games and the daily bonus surfaces are always free to enter. You never spend or stake coins to play, and you can never lose coins by playing. Coins leave your balance only when YOU request a payout, or when CPX reverses a survey credit as described above.

Variable bonuses and selection

Wheel, Scratch Card and Surprise Box outcomes are chosen on the server using configured reward tables. Visual animations reveal a result; they are not an independent proof of randomness or entitlement. The updated Reward rules control shows server-provided possible amounts, weights and calculated selection probabilities, daily limits and the 00:00 UTC reset.

A single configured outcome has a fixed amount. Approval is tied to the configuration; changes require review before new participation. These rules do not invent production probabilities. CPX participation additionally requires the provider's privacy/participation notices and qualifying-completion, screen-out, bonus and reversal terms.

KhelNest is offered to users in India; the app does not verify residence or legal eligibility. Free entry alone does not decide whether a cash-convertible mechanic is allowed by Google Play or local law, and this is not presented as a qualifying-transaction loyalty program.

No contests or tournaments

Players are never ranked against each other, and no reward here depends on beating anyone else. There is no leaderboard, no prize pool and no competition of any kind — what you earn depends only on your own play, within the published daily caps.

Payouts

The Wallet shows available NestCoin, survey-held amounts, payout reservations, the conversion rate, enabled payout methods and the minimum payout threshold. Depending on configured availability, methods include UPI transfer, PayPal or brand vouchers; do not assume bank-account transfer is available without a supported form.

A request reserves available coins and is reviewed manually; survey-held coins cannot be included before their individual unlock times. Payout requests and payments are processed manually and are not guaranteed; rejected or failed requests follow the server's refund transitions. A survey hold's expiry is not an automatic transfer or a 48-hour payment deadline.

Google Play credit and Google Play gift cards are not offered. We do not promise any particular income. Applicable payout limits, fees and method availability must be disclosed before a request; a timeout or unresolved request is not permission to submit duplicate payouts.

Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to earn or redeem. During sign-up you enter your date of birth and our servers work out your age from it — the app on your device cannot declare you old enough. A date below the minimum is refused and nothing is stored, and earning and redemption stay blocked until an accepted date of birth is on file.

No rewards for reviews

We will never ask you to rate or review the app for coins. Rating us is always optional and unrewarded.

Questions and disputes

Use Contact us at Contact us with your transaction/request reference and a description. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens. Google does not sponsor, guarantee or endorse KhelNest rewards. The operator must keep prior earned obligations and pending payouts accounted for when rules or backend projects change.
