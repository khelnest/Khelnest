1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old. During sign-up you enter your date of birth and our servers calculate your age from it; a date below the minimum is refused and is not stored, and earning and redemption stay blocked until an accepted date of birth is on file. We do not check that date against a document or an external service, so it remains something you tell us and you are responsible for its accuracy.

Accounts we reasonably believe to be fraudulent, or to belong to someone under 18, may be held, blocked, or closed.

2. How you earn

NestCoins are the app's reward units. Eligible gameplay, daily activities, free daily bonuses and qualifying referrals may earn NestCoins under server-managed rules and daily caps. The app displays the server balance; it cannot authorize a credit. Paid surveys and partner tasks are optional and currently disabled in the reviewed backend.

If activated, availability and qualifying actions must be disclosed before participation, and credits depend on the provider's verified completion and reversal rules.

3. Ads never pay coins

The current app does not offer rewarded videos for extra game hearts, puzzle moves, hints, or extra daily bonus attempts. Banner, native and interstitial ads may appear, subject to consent and availability; viewing or clicking them does not earn NestCoins. An ad is not required to release an already-earned balance or payout.

Any future rewarded benefit must remain separate from redeemable gameplay and cannot be transferred or converted into money or gift cards.

4. Free entry and variable daily bonuses

All games are FREE to enter. You never stake money or coins to play. NestCoins cannot be purchased, topped up or transferred to another user. The Wheel, Scratch Card and Surprise Box use server-managed reward tables; amounts may vary between tiers and daily limits apply. Free entry and the names of these features do not determine their legal or platform eligibility.

Reward selection, eligible markets and any applicable restrictions must be reviewed before these features are publicly released.

5. No contests, tournaments, or prizes

You are never ranked against other players and no reward depends on beating anyone. There is no leaderboard, no prize pool, and no competition of any kind. What you earn is decided only by your own play, within the published daily caps.

6. No paid reviews

We never offer coins or any reward in exchange for rating or reviewing the app on the Play Store.

7. NestCoins and redemption

NestCoins are redeemable reward units, not legal tender or a deposit account. Their conversion rate, payout methods, minimum threshold and applicable limits are displayed in the Wallet. A request locks the required coins while an operator reviews it. Approval and payout are not guaranteed; failed or rejected requests follow the server's refund rules.

Do not assume a particular processing time unless it is published for your payout method. Changes to future earning rules must not be described as settlement of existing approved obligations. Google Play credit and Google Play gift cards are not offered as a payout method.

8. Fair use and disputes

Automated play, multiple-account abuse, tampering and falsified completions are prohibited. Suspected abuse may result in a hold or restricted access while it is reviewed. Use Contact us at Contact us with the relevant transaction or request reference to dispute a decision; do not send a password or authentication token.

Any balance adjustment must have an explainable reason and follow the applicable published rules.

9. Closing your account

You can request deletion in Settings, then Account, then Delete account, or through the Mail link on Account deletion without installing the app. The current in-app queue has a 48-hour cancellation window. Your unredeemed balance is forfeited when account closure is completed, so resolve outstanding payout requests first.

Automated deletion is not enabled in the reviewed deployment. The current processor redacts selected app data but does not erase the Supabase Auth identity; a processing status alone is not complete account erasure. See the Privacy Policy for retained records and the current implementation limits.

10. Changes and contact

Material changes will be dated and communicated before they apply where required. Your statutory rights are not excluded by these terms. Questions about eligibility, rewards, privacy or account closure can be sent through Contact us at Contact us.
